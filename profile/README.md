# Neuromorphic Circuits & Systems

Paper digests and companion **RTL / Python** projects for neuromorphic and digital spiking neural network (SNN) circuits research.

**Affiliation:** SJTU Yongfu Research Group · Shanghai, China

## Focus

- Digital neuron cells (AdEx, LIF, Izhikevich, and related models)
- FPGA / ASIC-oriented RTL, Python goldens, and cosimulation
- Curated paper corpus with structured analyses and accessible explainers
